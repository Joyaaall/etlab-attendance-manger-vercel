Collegeil Pono: an Etlab attendance manager.

Collegeil pono? Check the numbers before you decide. Collegeil Pono helps students using Etlab see their attendance by subject, work out how many classes they can miss, and compare a full day off with an afternoon off.

Choose your college's Etlab portal above and sign in with your usual student login. The app reads your recorded attendance; it does not change your college records. It is an independent student project, not an official Etlab or college service.

How many classes can I miss at 75% attendance?

The answer depends on each subject's present and conducted hours. For example, 30 attended hours out of 36 lets you miss 4 more hours and finish at exactly 75%. A subject already at 75% has no hours to spare. You can change the target to match the percentage you want to check; 75% is a calculator default, not a claim about your college's rules.

Can I plan leave using my timetable?

Import your Etlab timetable or build a weekly timetable yourself. The leave planner checks full-day and after-lunch options across the next 14 days. If you select several dates, they share the same subject-wise attendance budget. Missing attendance or unassigned timetable periods block the affected option.

Does the app save my Etlab password?

No. Your password is used to sign in to your selected portal and is not saved by the application. Your active session stays in browser memory and clears when you refresh or sign out. Only your timetable and attendance-target preferences are saved in this browser.

Is a leave calculation permission to skip class?

No. These estimates use your current attendance and saved timetable. They do not count future classes you might attend or account for holidays, special schedules, mandatory labs, exemptions, or college-specific rounding. Check your official records and college rules before taking leave.
